## ⚙️ Game Features & Mechanics (v5.0)

### 1. Realistic Stamina & Form Floors
* Match fatigue is balanced (6%–12% per fixture based on intensity).
* Energy is strictly capped above a **35% stamina floor** and **30 form floor**, completely eliminating the bug where players become unusable after multiple seasons.
* Rested bench players regain **+18% to +28% stamina** each matchday.

### 2. Deep Stadium Complex & Multi-Purpose Arena
* **Multi-Purpose Arena & Expo Center:** Build an arena within your stadium complex to host concerts, international conventions, and expos, providing **€/£18,000 passive income every single week** regardless of whether you play home or away.
* **Pre-Match Light Show & Fan Pyrotechnics:** Laser shows, resident stadium DJs, and pyrotechnics that generate a cauldron atmosphere, boosting matchday attendance revenue by **+12%** and raising squad morale by **+3%**.
* **High-Performance Medical & Rehab Center:** Upgradable up to Level 50, providing up to **+8% extra weekly squad stamina recovery**.
* Upgrades for Hybrid Pitch, 4K LED Floodlights, VIP Luxury Suites, Youth Academy, and Naming Rights sponsorships (Regional, National, Global).

### 3. International Scouting & Elite Recruitment
* Dispatch scouts across 4 worldwide territories:
  1. **South America (Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Uruguay)**: Prodigious flair and high potential.
  2. **Continental Europe (France, Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium, Croatia)**: Tactical playmakers and physical dynamos.
  3. **Middle East & Asia (Saudi Arabia, Japan, South Korea, UAE)**: Marquee superstars and disciplined talents.
  4. **World Galácticos (Global)**: Elite world icons (Mbappé, Haaland, Vinícius Jr, Bellingham, Kane, Salah, De Bruyne, Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo).
* **2-Window Signing Window:** Scouted discoveries remain available in your recruitment shortlist for **2 full transfer windows**, allowing you to plan finances before signing.
* **One-Click Hiring Cards:** Sign scouted prospects directly from the Scouting tab or from Free Agents.

### 4. Smart AFK Auto Play
* Away-from-keyboard simulation designed for realistic multi-season speed runs:
  * Automatically calculates compound player ratings weighted by stamina and current form.
  * Dynamically switches between optimal formations (`4-4-2`, `4-3-3`, `3-5-2`, `4-2-3-1`, `5-3-2`) based on squad depth and injuries.
  * Rotates tired starters before stamina drops below fatigue thresholds.

### 5. Backroom Operations & Financial Integrity
* Option to offer confidential backroom incentives ("Match Bribes") to upcoming opponents of your title rivals.
* Risk-reward mechanic: UEFA / FA Financial Integrity investigators have an escalating probability of detecting match manipulation, resulting in severe sporting fines and point deductions.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Direct File Launch
Double-click `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave).

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended for PWA)
Run a local static server to test offline PWA installation:

Using Node.js:
```bash
npx serve .
# or
npx http-server -p 8080
```

Using Python:
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```
Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your browser.
